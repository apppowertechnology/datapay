How STRICTWALLET Works

Explore the exact real workflows for funding your wallet, purchasing data and airtime, hosting events, issuing tickets, verifying passes at event gates, and withdrawing proceeds.

Data & Airtime Wallet Funding Event Creation Ticket Purchasing Gate Scanning Withdrawals

A. Data & Airtime Purchase Flow

Instant automated dispatch via telecom provider API

Purchase mobile data bundles or airtime talk-time across all 4 Nigerian mobile networks (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile) with zero extra convenience surcharges on airtime.

STEP 1

Select Service & Network

Choose "Airtime Top-Up" or "Data Bundles" from the dashboard. Select your network (MTN, Airtel, Glo, or 9mobile).

STEP 2

Input Details

Enter the recipient 10- or 11-digit phone number. For airtime, enter the recharge amount (₦50 to ₦50,000). For data, select your preferred plan tier (SME, Corporate, or Direct).

STEP 3

Authorize with 4-Digit PIN

Enter your private 4-digit Transaction PIN. The balance is atomically debited and the provider API dispatches the bundle within seconds. Automatic refund applies if network fails.

B. Wallet Funding Process

Secure online card, USSD, and transfer checkout powered by Paystack

Fund your STRICTWALLET balance at any time from your computer or mobile phone. Minimum deposit is ₦100.

STEP 1

Initiate Deposit

Click "Fund Wallet" on your dashboard or select the top-up link during an event ticket purchase shortage. Enter the amount to fund (₦100 minimum).

STEP 2

Complete Paystack Checkout

The secure Paystack inline modal opens. Pay with your debit card, bank transfer, or USSD code through Paystack's encrypted gateway.

STEP 3

Instant Balance Update

Upon successful payment authorization, the system verifies the transaction reference and credits your wallet immediately with an audit entry in your ledger.

C. Event Creation & Publishing

Host events, upload artwork, configure tiers, and generate clean slugs

Event creators can build complete event listings in minutes with free publishing and automated URL slug generation.

STEP 1

Enter Event Details

Enter Title, Description, Venue location, Date, Time, and optional Ticket Sales Start/End window. Upload your promotional flyer and ticket background artwork.

STEP 2

Configure Ticket Tiers

Add one or more ticket tiers (e.g. Free, Early Bird, Regular, VIP, Table). Define the price (₦0 for free) and available seat capacity for each tier.

STEP 3

Publish & Share Link

Toggle status to "Published". Copy your unique shareable event link (strictwallet.com.ng/event/your-slug) to share across social media and flyers.

D. Ticket Purchase Flow

Secure ticket booking with cryptographic QR passes and PDF receipts

Customers discover events or click shared event links, pick their ticket tier, and complete purchases directly using their wallet balance.

STEP 1

Select Ticket Category

Open the event page, select the desired ticket category, and customize the attendee name on the ticket if purchasing for someone else.

STEP 2

Authorize with PIN

Enter your 4-digit PIN. The system debits your wallet, credits 97% to the creator's wallet (3% platform fee; 0% on free tickets), and updates inventory.

STEP 3

Receive QR E-Ticket & PDF

Your ticket is generated with a unique Ticket ID (e.g. TKT-XXXX) and anti-counterfeit QR code. View on your screen or download the high-res PDF.

E. Gate Entry QR Scanner Flow

In-browser smartphone camera scanning with live duplicate prevention

No expensive scanning hardware needed. Event organizers use our built-in web scanner on any smartphone at the entrance.

STEP 1

Open In-App Gate Scanner

The event creator navigates to the event in their dashboard, selects "Gate Scanner", and grants standard browser camera access.

STEP 2

Point Camera at QR Code

Scan the attendee's digital phone ticket or printed PDF. The scanner instantly decodes the cryptographic token and checks the backend verification route.

STEP 3

Instant Check-In Result

A valid ticket displays a green success screen showing Attendee Name and Ticket Category, immediately marking it as "used". Scanned duplicate passes are flagged in red.

F. Event Creator Bank Withdrawal Flow

Direct bank payout via Strowallet with live NUBAN name enquiry and stamp duty rules

Withdraw ticket proceeds directly to your commercial bank account with transparent fee accounting and official PDF receipts.

STEP 1

Select Bank & Verify Account

Select your commercial bank from the list. Enter your 10-digit NUBAN account number. Strowallet performs a live name enquiry to confirm your legal bank name.

STEP 2

Review Real-Time Fee Breakdown

Enter withdrawal amount. Transfer charge is flat ₦20. Stamp duty is ₦0 for withdrawals below ₦10,000, and ₦50 for withdrawals of ₦10,000+. Your bank receives the full requested principal.

STEP 3

Authorize & Download Receipt

Confirm with your 4-digit PIN. The payout dispatches to your bank immediately. View your on-screen receipt or download a formal PDF withdrawal audit receipt.
