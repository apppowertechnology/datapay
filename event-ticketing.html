The Complete Event Creator Lifecycle

1. Event Setup & Configuration

Enter your Event Title, detailed Description, physical Venue location, Event Date, and Time. Set an optional Ticket Sales Start & End window to automate sales cutoff.

  • Supports custom Event Flyer Banner
  • Custom Ticket Artwork for PDF background
  • Automatically assigns a unique Event ID (e.g. EVT-XXXX)

2. Creating Tickets & Setting Prices

Configure one or multiple ticket tiers. You decide the price and seat quantity limit for each tier:

  • Free Admission: ₦0 ticket price with 0% platform fee
  • Paid Categories: Set any price (e.g. Regular ₦2,000, VIP ₦10,000, Table ₦50,000)
  • Capacity Limits: Set maximum tickets per category to prevent overselling

3. Publishing & Shareable Links

Toggle event status between "Draft" and "Published". The system automatically generates a clean, URL-friendly slug based on your title.

  • Direct URL: strictwallet.com.ng/event/your-slug
  • One-click link copying to share on WhatsApp, Instagram, X, or promotional flyers
  • Directly accessible by any visitor on mobile or desktop without 404 errors

4. Ticket Purchases & Digital Receipts

Attendees select their ticket tier, specify attendee name, and confirm payment with their 4-digit PIN.

  • Atomic deduction from wallet balance
  • Unique Ticket ID generated (e.g. TKT-XXXX)
  • Downloadable high-resolution PDF ticket containing event artwork & QR pass

5. In-Browser Gate Scanner

No dedicated hardware required. Use any smartphone or tablet browser to scan attendee QR codes directly at your event entrance.

  • Instant verification against live database
  • Displays Attendee Name, Ticket Tier, and Status
  • Once scanned, ticket is marked as used to prevent duplicate entry

6. Real-Time Attendee Roster

View live attendee audit records directly from your Creator Dashboard:

  • Attendee Full Name & Buyer Email Address
  • Ticket Tier purchased & purchase timestamp
  • Gate entry status (Valid vs Checked-In)

Organizer Wallet & Bank Withdrawal Rules

Your event ticket revenue is completely segregated in an Organizer Wallet. Review our transparent fee breakdown and payout rules:

Commission on Ticket Sales

• 97% to Event Creator: Credited immediately upon ticket purchase to your Organizer Wallet.
• 3% STRICTWALLET Commission: Retained by the platform to cover server infrastructure and cryptographic verification services.
• Free Tickets: 0% platform fee.

Bank Payout Terms

• Transfer Fee: Flat ₦20.00 per withdrawal.
• Stamp Duty: ₦0.00 for withdrawals < ₦10,000; ₦50.00 statutory stamp duty for withdrawals ≥ ₦10,000.
• Disbursement: The creator's bank account receives 100% of the requested withdrawal amount.
• Live Verification: Instant NUBAN name enquiry ensures payout details are accurate.
