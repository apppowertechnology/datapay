2. User Accounts & Security

2.1. Eligibility: You must provide accurate, current, and complete information during registration, including your full legal name, valid email address, active phone number, and a secure Next-of-Kin security verification answer.

2.2. Account Credentials: You are responsible for safeguarding your login password and your 4-digit Transaction PIN. Any transaction authorized with your credentials or Transaction PIN is deemed to have been authorized by you.

2.3. Security Question: The name of your next of kin provided during registration serves as a secondary security factor for account verification and password resets. Keep this information confidential.

3. Digital Wallet Operations & Funding

3.1. Wallet Utility: Your STRICTWALLET account balance is a closed-loop electronic ledger designed specifically for purchasing digital telecommunication services (data and airtime) and event tickets hosted on the platform.

3.2. Wallet Funding via Paystack: Users fund their wallet through our integrated, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway (Paystack), supporting Nigerian debit/credit cards, bank transfers, and USSD. Minimum deposit amount is ₦100.00.

3.3. Immediate Crediting: Wallet deposits are credited automatically upon provider webhook confirmation. In the rare event of inter-bank delay, funds are credited once bank settlement is confirmed.

4. Telecommunication Services (Data & Airtime)

4.1. Automated Delivery: Airtime recharges and mobile data bundles are dispatched through our telecom gateway providers immediately upon PIN authorization.

4.2. Irreversibility: Due to the automated nature of telecommunications networks (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile), purchases delivered to the recipient phone number entered by the user cannot be reversed or refunded. Please verify phone numbers and data plan choices carefully before confirming.

5. Event Creation & Organizer Obligations

5.1. Legitimate Events: Event Creators may create and publish legitimate public or private events. Organizers warrant that all event titles, dates, descriptions, venues, and ticket prices are truthful and accurate.

5.2. Prohibited Content: Organizers shall not create events promoting illegal activities, fraudulent schemes, hate speech, or unauthorized fundraising. STRICTWALLET reserves the right to unpublish, suspend, or delete non-compliant events without prior notice.

5.3. Event Fulfillment: The Event Creator is solely responsible for organizing the event, securing venue access, admitting verified ticket holders, and managing event logistics. STRICTWALLET acts solely as a technology provider for ticketing and payment processing.

6. Ticket Purchases & Admittance

6.1. Electronic Tickets: Upon purchase confirmation, attendees receive a digital ticket containing a unique cryptographic QR verification token. Tickets can be displayed on-screen or downloaded as a PDF.

6.2. Venue Entry: At the event venue, the attendee must present the QR code for scanning. Each ticket QR pass can be verified and checked in exactly once. Once scanned by the organizer's scanner, the ticket is marked as used and cannot be reused.

7. Organizer Payouts, Fees & Stamp Duty

7.1. Ticket Commission: Paid tickets incur a 3% platform commission retained by STRICTWALLET upon purchase. The remaining 97% is instantly credited to the Event Creator's Organizer Wallet. Free admission tickets incur 0% commission.

7.2. Withdrawal Processing: Event Creators may withdraw their organizer wallet balance to any registered Nigerian commercial bank account in their name. Account name enquiry is performed to confirm account validity before disbursement.

7.3. Withdrawal Charges: A standard bank transfer fee of ₦20.00 is charged per withdrawal.

7.4. Statutory Stamp Duty: In accordance with Nigerian electronic banking and stamp duty regulations:

  • Withdrawals below ₦10,000.00 incur ₦0.00 stamp duty.
  • Withdrawals of ₦10,000.00 or more incur a statutory ₦50.00 stamp duty.

7.5. Net Payout: The exact principal withdrawal amount requested is disbursed to the organizer's bank account. Applicable withdrawal fees and stamp duty are deducted directly from the creator's wallet balance.
