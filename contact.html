Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is data and airtime delivered?

Delivery is fully automated. As soon as you confirm your purchase with your 4-digit Transaction PIN, our telecom gateway dispatches the order to your network (MTN, Airtel, Glo, or 9mobile) within seconds.

How do I fund my wallet?

You can fund your STRICTWALLET balance instantly through our integrated Paystack payment gateway. We support all Nigerian debit cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve), direct bank transfer, and USSD. Deposits reflect in your wallet balance immediately upon payment verification (minimum deposit ₦100).

How does Event Creator gate scanning work?

Event creators can open our built-in QR camera scanner on any smartphone browser directly from the STRICTWALLET dashboard. Point your phone camera at an attendee's QR ticket pass for instant validity confirmation and one-time entry check-in.

What are the fees for withdrawing event earnings?

Withdrawals incur a standard ₦20 bank transfer fee. In accordance with statutory regulations, withdrawals of ₦10,000 and above incur a ₦50 statutory stamp duty (withdrawals below ₦10,000 have ₦0 stamp duty). The exact withdrawal amount requested is sent to your bank.
