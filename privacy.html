Privacy & Trust

Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust are paramount to STRICTWALLET. This policy describes how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your personal information and transaction records.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

STRICTWALLET ("we", "us", "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of our registered users, event organizers, and attendees. We process your data transparently, securely, and strictly for the provision of our digital wallet, telecom, and event ticketing services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide you with secure, functional services:

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, and active telephone number provided during account registration.
  • Security Credentials: Encrypted password hashes, encrypted 4-digit Transaction PIN hashes, and your Next-of-Kin security verification answer. We do not store plaintext passwords or PINs.
  • Transaction Data: Records of wallet top-ups, airtime recharges, data purchases, ticket bookings, and organizer payout requests.
  • Banking Details (Event Creators Only): Destination bank name, bank account number, and verified account holder name required to disburse event withdrawal proceeds.
  • Technical Identifiers: Browser type, IP address, and device logs utilized for session authentication, fraud prevention, and system diagnostics.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is utilized solely for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To maintain and authenticate your account sessions securely;
  • To fulfill automated airtime and data bundle delivery to specified recipient numbers;
  • To issue unique cryptographic QR electronic tickets for events;
  • To facilitate name enquiry and direct bank disbursement for Event Creator withdrawals;
  • To communicate transaction receipts, account notifications, and support ticket updates;
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent activities or unauthorized access attempts.

4. Data Protection & Encryption

We employ robust industry-standard safeguards to secure your personal and financial data:

  • One-Way Cryptographic Hashing: Passwords and transaction PINs are irreversibly hashed using bcrypt before storage.
  • Secure Communications: All platform traffic is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Access Restrictions: Administrative access is strictly restricted, monitored, and guarded by multi-level authorization controls.

5. Sharing with Third-Party Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share minimal necessary data only with certified functional partners required to deliver our core services:

  • Payment Processors: Licensed gateways (e.g. Paystack) for processing incoming card transactions.
  • Banking & Payout Partners: Regulated financial infrastructure providers (e.g. Strowallet) for executing bank transfers and name verification.
  • Telecom Aggregators: Telecommunication API gateways for automated dispatch of data bundles and airtime top-ups across MTN, Airtel, Glo, and 9mobile.

6. Cookies & Client-Side Storage

STRICTWALLET utilizes local session storage and standard cookies solely for essential functional operations, including keeping you securely logged in, managing navigation state between views, and remembering pending ticket return targets.

7. Your Data Rights

You have the right to review your registered profile details, update your account password, update your Transaction PIN via settings, view your complete transaction history, and request account deactivation by contacting our support team.

8. Policy Updates & Inquiries

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. The date of the most recent revision will always be displayed at the top of this document. If you have questions regarding this policy or our data practices, please reach out through our Contact Page or via WhatsApp at 09161041419.
