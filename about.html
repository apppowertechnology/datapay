About STRICTWALLET

"Accessible. Secure. In Control." — STRICTWALLET is a modern digital platform designed to solve common digital payment frictions in Nigeria by uniting automated telecom utilities with full-featured event ticketing and creator payouts.

Create Free Account Explore All Services

The Problem We Solve

Everyday users in Nigeria frequently encounter delayed VTU deliveries, complicated wallet funding interfaces, and fragmented systems where utility recharges and event ticket management are disconnected. Additionally, independent event organizers often struggle with expensive ticketing platforms, complex gate verification hardware, and unclear withdrawal terms.

The STRICTWALLET Solution

STRICTWALLET provides a consolidated, reliable platform with two integrated pillars: fast automated data and airtime top-ups across all four telecom networks (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile), and a streamlined event ticketing system with in-browser smartphone QR gate scanning and transparent bank payouts.

Actual Capabilities of the Platform

The Digital Wallet System

Serves as the central transaction hub. Users fund their balance securely via Paystack, authorize payments with a private 4-digit PIN, and maintain complete audit logs of every deposit and spend.

Automated Telecom Utilities

Dispatches data bundles (SME, Corporate, Direct) and talk-time airtime across MTN, Airtel, Glo, and 9mobile within seconds, backed by automatic fail-safe wallet refunds.

The Event Creator Platform

Empowers creators to publish events, configure ticket tiers, issue cryptographic QR passes, verify attendees at the gate with any phone camera, and withdraw 97% of ticket sales directly to their bank account.

Our Core Operating Principles

Accessible

Simple, clean mobile-responsive interfaces designed for everyone. Minimum wallet deposit is just ₦100, airtime from ₦50, and event publishing is 100% free.

Secure

Every sensitive action is protected by a 4-digit Transaction PIN, one-way bcrypt password and PIN hashing, next-of-kin recovery verification, and unique QR tokens.

In Control

Transparent fee schedules, detailed transaction receipts, downloadable PDF audit reports, and direct control over your ticket pricing and bank payouts.
