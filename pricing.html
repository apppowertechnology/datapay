Detailed Event Creator Withdrawal Economics

When you request a payout from your Organizer Wallet, the system ensures your destination bank account receives the exact requested amount. All transfer fees and stamp duties are deducted from your wallet balance.

RULE 1: UNDER ₦10,000

Withdrawals Below ₦10,000

  • • Creator withdrawal charge: ₦20.00
  • • Statutory stamp duty: ₦0.00
  • • Total wallet deduction: Amount + ₦20.00
  • • Bank receives: Exact withdrawal amount
  • • Accounting: ₦20 charge - ₦10 provider fee = ₦10 platform profit
Example: Withdraw ₦5,000 → Bank receives ₦5,000 | Wallet deducted ₦5,020.
RULE 2: ₦10,000 & ABOVE

Withdrawals of ₦10,000 or More

  • • Creator withdrawal charge: ₦20.00
  • • Statutory stamp duty: ₦50.00
  • • Total wallet deduction: Amount + ₦70.00
  • • Bank receives: Exact withdrawal amount
  • • Accounting: ₦20 charge - ₦10 provider fee + ₦50 stamp duty = ₦60 platform retained profit
Example: Withdraw ₦10,000 → Bank receives ₦10,000 | Wallet deducted ₦10,070.
